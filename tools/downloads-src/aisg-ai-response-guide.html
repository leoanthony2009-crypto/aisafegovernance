AI safeguarding response guide

What every member of staff does when a concern involves AI — for all staff, supply staff and volunteers. DSL means Designated Safeguarding Lead.

Immediate action required

Child in immediate danger: call 999

Including an immediate risk of self-harm.

Every other concern

Tell the DSL — the same day

In person or by phone, not email alone. DSL and deputy unreachable and a child at risk? Contact children’s social care or the police yourself.

1Listen and reassure

“Thank you for telling me.” · “You are not in trouble.” · “This is not your fault.” · “We will get help.”

!

Do not promise to keep it secret. Say who you will tell and why.

2Don’t look, don’t copy

  • if shown an image, look away — you do not need to see it to report it
  • keep a device handed to you secure for the DSL, unviewed
  • do not view, ask for, forward, save, screenshot, print or copy imagery — even “as evidence”
  • do not delete anything, or tell a pupil to — deletion is the DSL’s decision
  • do not investigate, confront anyone or contact parents yourself

3Note what you heard

The pupil’s own words, the app or platform, usernames, date and time, what you did. Never a copy of the image; never speculation.

4Tell the DSL and record it

Record on the safeguarding system the same day. Threats or demands for money (sextortion): reach the DSL before the pupil goes home; advise the pupil to stop replying and not to pay.
