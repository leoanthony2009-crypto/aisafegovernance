Safeguarding recording checklist

What goes in the record, what never does, and when it must be done. Record on your school’s safeguarding system the same day.

Record — the same day

  • date, time and place of the disclosure or discovery
  • the pupil’s own words, as close to verbatim as you can
  • the app, platform or website, and any usernames mentioned
  • who or what was involved, as described to you
  • what you said and did, and when you told the DSL
  • any immediate action taken (999, children’s social care)

Never record or keep

  • do not keep a copy, screenshot, print or description-by-viewing of any image
  • do not record your theories about who made or shared anything
  • do not record notes from questioning other pupils — you don’t investigate
  • do not promise or record promises of confidentiality
!

Tell the DSL in person or by phone first. The written record follows the conversation — it never replaces it.

Keep facts to what safeguarding needs — no more. Data minimisation protects the pupil and the record.
