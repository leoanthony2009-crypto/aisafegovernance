School guide to Report Remove

How a pupil under 18 can ask for a nude or sexual image of them online to be assessed for removal, and the school’s role around it.

Report Remove is run by Childline and the Internet Watch Foundation (IWF). This is an independent educational guide to it — not an NSPCC, Childline or IWF publication.

What it is

A young person under 18 in the UK can report a nude or sexual image or video of themselves that is online — including AI-made or altered imagery — so the IWF can assess it and work to have it removed. The pupil signs in or creates a Childline account at childline.org.uk (search “Report Remove”) and gets updates in their Childline locker.

!

Removal is not guaranteed and timing varies. It cannot reach encrypted apps such as WhatsApp, or files stored only on a device.

The school’s role

  • tell the DSL first — the DSL decides with the pupil, and a parent or carer where safe, whether and when to use it
  • the pupil reports through their own Childline account on their own device, with a trusted adult’s support where needed
  • record that Report Remove was discussed — removal is one strand of the safeguarding response, not the whole of it
  • the adult never views, handles or uploads the image
  • staff never make the report for the pupil, or use a school device or account
