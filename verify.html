What a match tells you

The credential ID is worked out from the course, the learner’s name and the completion date. A match shows the three printed details belong together and have not been edited apart. It is a consistency check, not a cryptographic signature, and it does not confirm the learner’s identity: AISG does not store learners’ details, because the course keeps everything in the learner’s own browser.

The course is a CPD record of completion, not an accredited or regulated qualification. Questions: aisafegovernance.co.uk
