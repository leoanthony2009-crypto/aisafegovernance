AI tool check

AI tool pre-adoption check

You are looking at the specimen. A completed check of an invented product, so you can see how the outcome and records work. It cannot be changed.

Check a named AI tool before staff or pupils use it

The check takes the tool through the DfE's 13 Generative AI product safety standards, one standard at a time, rated on the evidence the supplier and your own trial can actually show. It produces an outcome the DSL and DPO can act on, a letter of questions for the supplier, and a decision record for governors.

  1. Product details. Name the tool, who will use it and where its data goes.
  2. Review. Work through the standards that apply. Critical controls need evidence on file.
  3. Act and record. Complete the school actions, then copy the records.

Review

Product details

Who uses the tool decides which standards apply. If pupils use it, all 13 apply. If staff use it but pupils see what it produces, or it handles pupil data, 9 apply. For staff-only tools with no pupil data, 8 apply.

Administration

Save & open a check

Checks are saved in this browser only. To move a check to another device or keep a copy, copy its save code. On a shared computer, remove the check when you finish.

Anyone with the save code can read the check, so keep names and pupils' details out of it.

Administration

Moderated check

An optional paid service. Booking does not change the outcome shown by this tool.

  • AISG reviews your check and the supplier's evidence against each critical control.
  • If the supplier provides a trial account, AISG runs the trial tests described in each standard.
  • You receive written findings and a record marked "Moderated by AISG: evidence reviewed on [date]". This is not a certification or endorsement. The school still makes the decision.
