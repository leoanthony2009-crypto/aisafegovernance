Why this update

Staff now meet AI incidents — AI-generated nude images, companion apps, disclosures made to chatbots — without a clear response route. International safeguarding guidance names these risks within the 4Cs of online safety, and in Trinidad and Tobago they sit inside duties staff already hold under the Children Act 2012 and the Sexual Offences Act. This update gives every member of staff the same calm route: recognise, respond, record, refer.

“This is a specialist safeguarding awareness and update session.
