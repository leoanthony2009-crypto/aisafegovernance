Why this update

Staff now meet AI incidents — AI-generated nude images, companion apps, disclosures made to chatbots — without a clear response route. Keeping Children Safe in Education 2026 (in force 1 September 2026) names these risks within the 4Cs of online safety. This update gives every member of staff the same calm route: recognise, respond, record, refer.
